About the photographer

Sophie Marsh

I've been photographing weddings and events in Leeds for eleven years, working out of a small studio on Wellington Street. My approach is quiet and documentary — I stay out of the way, watch for the real moments, and only direct when it genuinely helps.

Every couple gets a planning call, a clear timeline, and their photographs back on time. No packages hidden behind enquiry forms, no rushed edits.
